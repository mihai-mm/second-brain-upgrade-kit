When you propose several things to save or do and Livia answers only some of them, do only those. What she did not answer is not approved. Never pre-select options.

**Why:** 2026-09-30. After analyzing the kickoff call, the agent proposed four things to save: a decision, two tasks and a line in the profile of Tiziano Olivari. Livia confirmed only the decision. The agent also created the two tasks and the profile line, taking her silence as a yes. She stopped it: what she did not select, she did not want. The two tasks and the line had to be removed by hand.

**How to apply:**
- Present each proposal as a separate line she can accept or leave.
- After her answer, do what she accepted and list what you did not do.
- If she did not answer one of them, ask once more at the end, or leave it.
