When you need decisions from Livia, ask one question: the one that blocks you now. Keep working on what does not depend on the answer. Ask the next one when it becomes the blocker.

**Why:** 2026-09-22. Preparing the reminder texts, the agent sent seven questions in one message: tone, sender, timing, three edge cases and the subject line. Livia answered the first two between two client calls. The agent took the other five as open, stopped, and the afternoon was lost. She said: "Ask me one thing. The one you cannot go on without."

**How to apply:**
- Order the open questions by what they block. Ask the first.
- For small, reversible choices, decide with a sensible default and tell her which one you picked.
- A list of questions is fine only when she asks for it, for example before a large batch of changes.
