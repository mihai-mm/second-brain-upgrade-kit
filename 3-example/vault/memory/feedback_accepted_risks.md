Flag a risk once, clearly. After Livia says she accepts it, do not raise it again. It comes back only if a new fact changes it, and then you say what the new fact is.

**Why:** 2026-10-07. The reminder emails for Calvesi Accounting go out from the firm's general address, because Ottavio Calvesi wanted it that way. The agent pointed out three times, in three sessions, that replies would land in a shared inbox. The third time Livia answered: "I know. Ottavio decided, and I accepted it. Stop bringing it up." The warning had become noise, and it was hiding the warnings that were new.

**How to apply:**
- Before raising a risk, check `decisions.md` of the project and this index: was it already accepted?
- If it was, stay silent, unless something changed. Then open with the change: "New fact since you accepted this: ...".
- An accepted risk is recorded in the project's `decisions.md`, with the date and who accepted it.
