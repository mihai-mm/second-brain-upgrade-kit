name: plain-words-clients
description: In any text for a client, say what happens for them, not how it is built
type: feedback
---

In texts for a client, describe what happens in their office in plain words. Do not describe how the system is built, and do not use technical terms the client has not used.

**Why:** 2026-10-02. The agent drafted a message for Ottavio Calvesi that spoke of "automation", "trigger" and "workflow". Livia rewrote it as "a message goes out on its own seven days after the due date, and another after twenty-one". She said the owner of a small firm does not read about triggers, he reads about his Mondays. The first version would have gone unread.

**How to apply:**
- Use the client's own words for the things they already name: invoice, reminder, mailbox.
- If a technical term is unavoidable, explain it in half a sentence the first time.
- Keep the technical detail in the project files, for Livia.
