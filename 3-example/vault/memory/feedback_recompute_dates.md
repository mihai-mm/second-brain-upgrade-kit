---
name: recompute-dates
description: Recompute today's date before writing any relative date in a text that leaves the vault
type: feedback
---

Before writing "today", "tomorrow", "tonight" or a day of the week in a message or a document that goes out, check today's date and recompute the reference against the date of the event. Do not copy the words from an earlier note.

**Why:** 2026-10-06. In a draft email for Ottavio Calvesi the agent wrote "see you tomorrow" about the call of Thursday, October 8. It had copied the sentence from a note written the day before. The call was two days away, not one. Livia caught it before sending and said it was not the first time something like this happened.

**How to apply:**
- Look up today's date from the session context or with the `date` command.
- When in doubt, write the full date: "Thursday, October 8, at 10:00".
- Check every relative date in a draft, also the ones in text you were given.
