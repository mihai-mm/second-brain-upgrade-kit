When you deliver more than Livia asked for, list the additions in one line at the top. Extras are welcome. Silent extras are not.

**Why:** 2026-09-24. Livia asked for the list of fields needed from the invoicing software export. The agent delivered the list, plus a proposed structure for the data and a paragraph on how long to keep it. The paragraph was reasonable. Two days later she found it, almost word for word, inside a draft email to Tiziano Olivari, and had to work out where it came from and whether she had ever agreed to it.

**How to apply:**
- First line of the answer: "Beyond what you asked, I added: ...".
- Keep the additions separable, so she can drop them without touching the rest.
- If the addition is a decision that belongs to her (scope, promises to a client), propose it instead of writing it in.
