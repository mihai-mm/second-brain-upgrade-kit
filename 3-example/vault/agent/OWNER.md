# Livia Lanteri - profile

Example file, all content invented. Lasting facts only, loaded in every session. Where a project stands is not here.

## Professional identity

Independent automation consultant for small professional firms: accountants, dentists, lawyers. She has worked alone since 2023. Her clients have a few employees, one person who handles the administration, and no technical staff.

She is not a developer by training. She decides what gets built, for whom and in which order, and she is the one who talks to clients. The agent writes the code and the documents.

Her clients read little and answer when something is simple and short.

## Values

- Plain over impressive. A client should understand what was built in one minute.
- Small and finished beats large and promised.
- Nothing goes out to a client's customers that the client has not approved in writing.
- Honesty about what is not known. "I don't know yet" is acceptable. A guess presented as a fact is not.

## How she works

- Works alone, in short sessions, often interrupted by client calls. Each session must be resumable from the log.
- Decides quickly when the options are laid out as before and after. Slows down when she has to rebuild the text in her head.
- Prefers a trial on a small list chosen by the client before anything touches all clients.
- Reads drafts line by line and rewrites what does not sound like her. Corrections to the agent are given in the moment and meant to be saved.
- Dislikes hype, long preambles and answers that start by praising the question.

## Where the current state lives (not in this file)

- Where each project stands: current state at the top of the latest log of that project, `projects/<name>/log/`. Entry point: [Home.md](../Home.md).
- Decisions with their reasons: `decisions.md` of the project.
- Her task list: the task app.
- What changed this week or this month: not here. If a dated status appears in this file, it is out of place and must be moved or removed.

## Details: when to read them

Read [OWNER-details.md](OWNER-details.md) when you need to:

- write to a client or take part in a client conversation on her behalf;
- help her decide between options, or explain a trade-off;
- notice why a request is going in circles or a project is slowing down.

It is not needed for a technical edit or a question about a file.

## Constraints that always hold

- Credentials stay out of code and documents.
- A client's customer names stay inside that client's project folder.
- Messages to third parties are sent only with her explicit go-ahead.
- She is the only person who writes the style file. The agent proposes, she confirms.
- Questions to her one at a time, with the options written out in the chat.

## How she decides

- Takes a project only if there is a named person on the client side who answers within two working days (decision of September 2026, see [the decision](knowledge/decisions/2026-09-named-contact-on-every-project.md)).
- Chooses the option that can be undone over the one that is slightly better.
- A risk she has accepted once is not raised again unless a new fact arrives.
- Wants the plan of a longer job written before the first step.
