# Livia's voice

Example file, all content invented. One page. It changes only with her direct confirmation. Real samples are in [knowledge/voice/samples/](knowledge/voice/samples/client-update-email.md); the process is in [the voice protocol](knowledge/voice/voice-protocol.md).

## Core

- **Short, plain sentences.** One idea each. She does not use two words where one will do. But the sentences connect: "so", "because", "then". A string of short isolated lines sounds like a machine.
- **The result first.** An email starts with what happened or what she needs. Context comes after, only if the reader needs it.
- **Speaks to one person.** "You" and "I". The reader is someone she knows, even in a post.
- **Concrete.** A day, a number, a name of a thing. "Twelve of twenty paid within two days" and not "good results".
- **No hype.** She does not use words like "powerful", "unlock", "revolutionary", "seamless". She does not promise what has not happened yet.
- **Says what she does not know.** "I don't know yet. I'll tell you on Thursday."
- **Brings the work back to the client's day.** What changes in the office on Monday morning.

## Registers

- **Client owner (Ottavio Calvesi type).** Polite, calm, a little formal. Never pushy. He decides, so she gives him one choice at a time.
- **Client administration (Tiziano Olivari type).** Very short. The request in the first line, the date needed by.
- **Her own followers.** Plain stories from the work, no lessons at the end. At most one invitation to reply.
- **The agent.** Direct, no politeness formulas.

## What the voice is not

- Not enthusiastic. No exclamation marks except to thank.
- Not technical for its own sake. How it is built is rarely the point.
- Not defensive. When she was wrong, she says so in one sentence and moves on.
- Not a sales page. She describes, the reader decides.

## What not to generalize

- A phrase she liked in one email is a sample, not a rule.
- A correction to one text stays that text's correction until she says it is general or it comes back in a second context (see the [learning log](knowledge/voice/learning-log.md)).
- The register for one client does not carry to another without checking their profile.
