# Calvesi Accounting

Example profile: the firm and the people are invented. What lasts over time, to read before writing to the firm or on its behalf. Where the work stands is not here: it lives in the log of the project.

## Who they are

An accounting firm with eight people. Ottavio Calvesi is the owner and decides. [Tiziano Olivari](tiziano-olivari.md) runs the administration and is the daily contact. The firm's clients are small businesses and self-employed people, many of whom have known the firm for years.

Livia has worked with the firm since September 29, 2026. Project: `projects/calvesi-accounting/` (see its index, [calvesi-accounting.md](../../../projects/calvesi-accounting/calvesi-accounting.md)).

## How they communicate

- Email, from the firm's administration address. Ottavio Calvesi reads and answers in the evening.
- Ottavio Calvesi approves in writing every text that goes to the firm's clients. A phone yes is confirmed by a short message afterwards.
- Short messages with one request each. Long attachments are not read: put three lines in the body.
- Ottavio Calvesi prefers one choice at a time, with Livia's recommendation first.

## Constraints and habits

- Nothing goes to the firm's clients without Ottavio Calvesi's written yes.
- Month-end and tax deadlines are the busy periods. Non-urgent requests go out before the 25th of the month.
- The firm wants a written trace of anything sent to clients, kept in its own mailbox. It does not want conversations with clients on personal phones.
- The names of the firm's clients stay in the project folder. They do not go into profiles, memory or examples.

## Things to know

- **2026-10-06** Ottavio Calvesi judges a message that sounds pushy as a risk to the relationship with long-standing clients. He prefers fewer messages with a polite tone.
- **2026-10-01** The firm does not keep an up-to-date list of who is late. The knowledge is in Tiziano Olivari's head.
