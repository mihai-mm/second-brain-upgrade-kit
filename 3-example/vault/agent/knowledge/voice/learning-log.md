# Voice learning log

Example file, all content invented. Every correction Livia makes to a draft goes here as a case, before it becomes an instruction. This keeps the style file from growing by accumulation. Do not delete rejected cases: they stop a false rule from coming back.

Scope values: **this text only**, **this kind of text**, **always**. A case becomes "always" only if Livia says so or it recurs in two independent contexts.

## Cases

### 2026-09-30 - Update email to a client owner

- **Kind of text:** email, update, to the owner of an accounting firm.
- **What the agent wrote:** opened with "I hope you are well" and a paragraph on what the project is about, then the news in the third paragraph.
- **What Livia changed:** deleted the first two paragraphs. The email now starts with "The kickoff went well. Here is what we agreed."
- **Rule learned:** an update to a client opens with the result or the decision. Context comes after, and only if the reader needs it.
- **Scope:** this kind of text.

### 2026-10-05 - Post for her followers

- **Kind of text:** LinkedIn post, a short story from client work.
- **What the agent wrote:** "Unlock the power of automation: how a small firm took back its Mondays."
- **What Livia changed:** rewrote the opening as "A small firm told me they find out who is late at the end of the month." Removed the title with the promise.
- **Rule learned:** no promise words and no slogans in the title. The post starts from something that happened.
- **Scope:** always. She said so directly: "I don't write like that, ever."

### 2026-10-09 - Short reply to the administration contact

- **Kind of text:** short email, a request, to the head of administration of a client.
- **What the agent wrote:** "I would be very grateful if, when you have a moment, you could send me the list of clients for the trial."
- **What Livia changed:** "Can you send me the list of the twenty clients by Monday?"
- **Rule learned:** ask in the first line, with the item and the date. No cushioning.
- **Scope:** this kind of text, for this recipient. It matches his profile: the check is whether the same holds for other administration contacts.

### 2026-10-12 - Update email to a client owner

- **Kind of text:** email, update, to the owner of an accounting firm, before a trial.
- **What the agent wrote:** "You will have the results by Friday."
- **What Livia changed:** "I will write to you when the trial is over, with the numbers."
- **Rule learned:** no date or promise that she has not given herself. If the date is not hers, say what happens and not when.
- **Scope:** always. Dates and promises in her name come from her.
