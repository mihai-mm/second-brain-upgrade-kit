An accounting firm told me they find out who is late with an invoice at the end of the month. By then it is three weeks late.

Nobody was ignoring it. The list of who owes what was in one person's head. She knew it by heart. She had just never written it down.

So we started small. We wrote the list. We wrote two short messages, one after a week and one after three. The owner read both and changed two words.

I don't know yet if it works for every client. We will try it on twenty and look at the numbers.

What I have noticed so far: the hard part was not the tool. It was getting the list out of a head and onto a page.

If you run a small firm, who keeps your list in their head?
