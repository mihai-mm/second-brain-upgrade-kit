# Calvesi Accounting - decisions

The decisions made with their reason, what has been learned, the numbers that last. The state of the work lives in the log.

## Decisions

- **2026-10-08** The reminders go out by email, from the firm's administration address. Reason: the firm wants a written trace in its own mailbox and doesn't want conversations with clients on personal phones. Supersedes the September 29 decision on the channel. Detail: call of October 8.
- **2026-10-06** Only two reminders: at 7 and at 21 days after the due date. After the second, the case goes to a person. Reason: Ottavio Calvesi judged a third automatic message too pushy for this kind of clientele.
- **2026-09-29** We start from the reminders, not from rebuilding the management software. Reason: it is the job that costs administration the most time today, and it can be measured in a few weeks. Review at the end of the trial.
- **2026-09-29** ~~The reminders go out on WhatsApp.~~ Superseded on October 8: see that day's decision. It had been chosen because clients answer faster.

## Things learned

- **2026-10-01** The delay in sending reminders isn't a matter of will: nobody at the firm has an up-to-date list of who is late. They find out at the end of the month, looking at the account statement.
- **2026-10-15** The clients who pay after the first reminder are almost all those with a single overdue invoice. Those with three or more don't answer automatic messages.

## Numbers

- Overdue invoices at September 30, 2026: 64, from 41 clients. Source: export received on October 1.
- Trial on 20 clients, October 13 to 15, 2026: 12 paid within two days of the reminder, 3 answered asking for more time, 5 didn't answer. Source: trial results.
